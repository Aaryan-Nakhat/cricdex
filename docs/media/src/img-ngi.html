📈 Net Game Impact (NGI)

Who really won it? Credit every ball by the win it moved.

A win-probability model reads the match before every delivery. The change in win chance after the ball is that ball's value: plus to the batter, minus to the bowler, with a share to the fielders on catches and run outs.
